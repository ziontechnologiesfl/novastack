KS Skincare does not use third‑party analytics tools, advertising networks, or other SDKs that collect personal data.

4. Device Information

We do not intentionally collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or technical information that can identify you as an individual. Any data processed temporarily by app stores, your device, or your network provider is handled under their own policies (for example, Google Play or your device manufacturer).

5. Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through any external channel (such as email or support forms), please contact us so that we can delete it.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change how the app works or start collecting any data, we will update this Privacy Policy and, where required, notify users through the app or the app store listing before the changes take effect.

Content in the app is for general skincare education and self‑care guidance. It is not intended to:

Consult a Professional

Always consult a qualified dermatologist or healthcare professional before starting any new skincare routine, especially if you have allergies, existing conditions, sensitive skin, or are using prescription treatments.
